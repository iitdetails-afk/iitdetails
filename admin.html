📁 Connect Google Sheets on Google Drive
Quick 2-minute setup to connect and manage your 3 separate sheets
✨ How it works:

This Apps Script runs inside your Google Spreadsheet on Google Drive. It automatically creates, updates, and formats 3 separate sheets (Staff, Students, and Addresses) whenever changes occur.

1
Go to Google Drive and create a New Google Sheet (e.g. named "IIT Database Master").
2
Inside your Google Sheet, click the top menu: Extensions > Apps Script.
3
Erase any existing code, copy the script below and paste it in.
4
Click the blue Deploy button (top right) > New deployment.
5
Click the ⚙️ icon next to "Select type" > choose Web app.
6
Set Execute as: "Me" and Who has access: "Anyone", then click Deploy.
7
Copy the resulting Web app URL, paste it into the Web App URL box and click Push All to Google Sheets!
